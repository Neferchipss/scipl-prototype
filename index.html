How we work

From first survey to the end of the defects liability period.

01

Survey & measure

Site visit, measured survey and a written understanding of the brief before anything is priced.

02

Budget & BOQ

A line-by-line bill of quantities against the architect's drawings, with alternatives where they save money.

03

Mobilisation

Approvals, permits, hoarding, site set-up and induction — the week that decides the programme.

04

Execution

Our own supervisors on site daily, trades sequenced in parallel, quality checked against the specification.

05

Handover & after

Snagging closed before handover, then a defined defects liability period with a named point of contact.
